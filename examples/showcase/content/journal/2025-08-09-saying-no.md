---
title: The Power of Saying No
date: 2025-08-09
---

Today, I had to decline a request that didn't align with my priorities. It felt uncomfortable at first, but ultimately liberating.

> "The difference between successful people and really successful people is that really successful people say no to almost everything." - Warren Buffett

**Small learning:** Saying no to things that don't serve your goals creates space for things that do. Protect your time and energy.
