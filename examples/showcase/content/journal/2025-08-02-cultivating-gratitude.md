Today, I took a moment to reflect on things I'm grateful for. It's amazing how a shift in perspective can change everything.

> "Gratitude turns what we have into enough, and more. It turns denial into acceptance, chaos into order, confusion into clarity... It makes sense of our past, brings peace for today, and creates a vision for tomorrow." - Melody Beattie

**Small learning:** Practicing gratitude daily can significantly improve mental well-being and overall happiness.
