---
title: The Importance of Self-Compassion
date: 2025-08-06
---

Today was a tough day, and I found myself being overly critical. I realized the importance of treating myself with the same kindness I'd offer a friend.

> "If your compassion does not include yourself, it is incomplete." - Jack Kornfield

**Small learning:** Self-compassion isn't self-pity; it's recognizing our shared humanity and allowing ourselves grace, especially during difficult times.
