date: 2025-08-12

Today, I was struck by how effectively a complex idea was conveyed through a simple story. It made me reflect on my own communication.

> "After nourishment, shelter and companionship, stories are the thing we need most in the world." - Philip Pullman

**Small learning:** Stories resonate deeply with people. Whether in presentations, code documentation, or everyday conversations, a well-told story can make information memorable and impactful.
