---
title: Embracing Imperfection
date: 2025-07-30
---

Today was a reminder that perfection is often the enemy of good. Sometimes, it's better to ship something imperfect than to wait indefinitely for flawless.

> "Have no fear of perfection - you'll never reach it." - Salvador Dalí

**Small learning:** Iteration and feedback are more valuable than striving for an unattainable ideal from the start. Progress over perfection.
