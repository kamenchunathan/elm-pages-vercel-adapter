date: 2025-07-26

Today, I was reminded of the power of simplicity. In a world that often glorifies complexity, finding elegance in simple solutions is a true art.

> "Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication." - Leonardo da Vinci

**Small learning:** Sometimes, the best way to solve a complex problem is to break it down into its simplest components. Over-engineering can be a trap.
