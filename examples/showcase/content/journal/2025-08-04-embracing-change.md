title: Embracing Change
date: 2025-08-04
---

Change is constant, and today was a good reminder to embrace it rather than resist it. Adaptability is a superpower.

> "The only way to make sense out of change is to plunge into it, move with it, and join the dance." - Alan Watts

**Small learning:** Resisting change often leads to stagnation. Being open to new ideas and approaches can lead to unexpected opportunities.
